The Problem
IBM's most valuable asset — its people's contributions — is largely invisible until it's too late
The average employee spends 20+ hours per year preparing for performance reviews, and 73% say they struggle to articulate their achievements — not because the work wasn't done, but because the evidence of that impact is scattered across Jira, Slack, email, and memory. Leaders make promotion, recognition, and resource decisions based on what they remember rather than what actually happened.
Without Clarity — Today
⏱Employees spend 20+ hours per year reconstructing accomplishments from scattered tools and memory
📉Recency bias dominates — Q4 is remembered, Q1 is forgotten, even if Q1 was more impactful
🔗Work–goal mapping is 100% manual — employees copy-paste from tools into SuccessFactors with no AI assistance
🚫No private career planning space — employees can't track their own growth trajectory without it being visible to managers
📊Leaders make promotion and resource decisions based on incomplete, anecdotal evidence rather than continuous data
😞73% of employees feel their contributions aren't fully visible to leadership — reducing engagement and increasing attrition risk
With Clarity
⚡Work logged in under 30 seconds — or auto-drafted from Jira and Slack activity with one-click acceptance
🤖IBM watsonx AI automatically derives skills demonstrated, impact created, and measurable metrics from every log entry
🎯Every entry is AI-mapped to a SuccessFactors goal in real time — with flags when goals are at risk of going unevidenced
🗺The Career Navigator builds a personalised, gap-based roadmap from the employee's own logged history — private, actionable, AI-powered
📋Leaders get a continuous, evidence-based view of team contributions — enabling fair, defensible promotion and development decisions
📝Performance review package generated in one click — instead of reconstructing a year, employees are continuously building their future
The root cause: IBM employees have SuccessFactors for goals and Jira/Slack/email for work — but no intelligent layer that connects the two continuously. Clarity is that missing layer, built entirely on IBM watsonx, turning daily work into a living record of professional growth.

Beyond cost savings: leadership, retention & engagement
Evidence-based promotions
Leaders access a continuous, AI-maintained record of every team member's contributions — making promotion decisions faster, fairer, and more defensible. No more relying on memory or recency.
Career motivation & engagement
Employees with a visible, AI-maintained career roadmap are 2.4× more likely to stay engaged (Gallup). Clarity makes IBM's goals and the employee's personal goals the same thing.
Reduced attrition risk
Career stagnation is the #1 reason high performers leave. A private growth engine tied to daily work directly addresses the retention risk — without surveillance or added management overhead.
The compounding effect: When an employee logs work that maps to a SuccessFactors goal and a personal career milestone simultaneously, they experience direct, daily evidence that IBM's success and their own growth are the same thing. This alignment drives the discretionary effort — the work people choose to do beyond their job description — that is the source of IBM's competitive edge. Clarity makes that alignment visible, measurable, and continuous.
