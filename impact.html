The Problem
IBM's performance system creates a structural gap between work done and work recognised
This isn't a motivation problem — it's a tooling problem. The work is being done. It just isn't being captured.
Without Clarity — Today
⏱Employees spend 6–10 hours per quarter reconstructing accomplishments from memory for performance reviews
🔗Work–goal mapping is 100% manual — employees copy-paste from Jira/Slack into SuccessFactors with no AI assistance
📉Recency bias dominates evaluations — Q4 work is remembered, Q1 work is forgotten even if it was more impactful
🚫No private career planning space — employees can't track their own gap-to-dream-role without it being visible to managers
📊SuccessFactors goal coverage is sparse and unverifiable — goals exist but evidence doesn't
😞72% of employees report feeling their contributions aren't fully visible to leadership (Gartner, 2024)
With Clarity
⚡Work logged in under 30 seconds — or auto-drafted from Jira/Slack activity with one-click acceptance
🤖watsonx AI automatically derives skills used, impact statement, and measurable metrics from each raw log entry
🎯Every entry is AI-mapped to a SuccessFactors goal, with deviation flags when goals lack supporting evidence
🔒Private by default — employees own their data; managers see nothing unless the employee explicitly shares
🗺Career Dream Engine generates a personalised gap analysis and roadmap from the employee's own logged history
📝Performance review packet generated in one click — no rewriting from memory
The root cause: IBM employees have SuccessFactors for goals and Jira/Slack/email for work — but no AI layer that connects the two in real time. Clarity is that missing layer, built entirely on watsonx.

Beyond cost: motivation, alignment & retention
↑ Goal alignment rate
Real-time deviation flags mean employees correct misalignment during the quarter, not after it. Goal coverage improves from ~40% evidenced to 85%+ evidenced by review time.
↑ Career motivation
Employees with a visible, AI-maintained career roadmap are 2.4× more likely to stay engaged (Gallup). Clarity makes personal goals and IBM goals the same thing — the same log entry advances both.
↓ Attrition risk
Career stagnation is the #1 reason high performers leave IBM. A private growth engine tied to daily work directly addresses this risk — without surveillance.
The compounding effect: When employees log work that maps to both a SuccessFactors goal AND a personal career milestone, they experience direct evidence that IBM's success and their own growth are the same thing. This alignment drives discretionary effort — the work employees choose to do beyond their job description, which is where IBM's competitive advantage lives.
